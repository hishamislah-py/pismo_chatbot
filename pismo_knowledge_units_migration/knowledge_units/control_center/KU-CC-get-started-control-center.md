# Get started with Control Center

Use Control Center to build and manage your payment, banking, and lending programs on the Pismo platform through an interactive user interface. You can perform a variety of tasks, including:

* Create credit and debit card products
* Configure settings that apply across programs, such as fee models, flex controls, and processing codes
* Create and manage banking products, including savings and time deposit products
* Build lending products and manage customer loans
* Administer your customers’ accounts, including reissuing their cards
* Create, view, and manage cardholder transaction disputes
* Manage user access, such as creating users and assigning roles and permissions

The following guides help you get started:

[Sign on to Control Center](https://developers.pismo.io/pismo-docs/docs/get-access-and-sign-on)—Get access and sign on for the first time.

[Navigate Control Center](https://developers.pismo.io/pismo-docs/docs/control-center-navigation)—Explore the layout and key features.

[Control Center security](https://developers.pismo.io/pismo-docs/docs/security-for-control-center)—Learn how security policies protect access and data.
